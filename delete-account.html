Delete Account

Aquarist - Aquarium Tracker & Log

Effective Date: April 21, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Aquarist account and the associated data. Aquarist is developed by Yaşar Taymaz.

1. Delete from within the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the app:

  1. Open the Aquarist app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action when prompted.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer to request deletion by email, send a message to:

yasartaymaz@windowslive.com

Please include the email address associated with your Aquarist account and use the subject line "Delete Account Request". We will process your request within 14 days and reply to confirm.

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is removed:

  • Profile information (email, display name, avatar)
  • Aquarium tanks and their details
  • Maintenance logs, water parameters, and notes
  • Uploaded photos
  • Reminders and scheduled notifications
  • Push notification tokens
  • Sharing invitations you created

4. Data Retention

After account deletion, your account enters a 30-day recovery period during which the account can be reactivated by contacting support. After 30 days, all personal data listed above is permanently deleted from our systems.

Aggregate, anonymized analytics data that cannot be linked back to your identity may be retained for statistical purposes. This data contains no personal identifiers.

In rare cases, we may retain limited records for a longer period where required by law (for example, to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes). Such data is stored securely and is not used for any other purpose.

5. Shared Content

If you previously shared a tank with another Aquarist user, ownership of that shared tank is transferred to the other user before deletion. Your personal identifiers are removed from any shared context.

6. Questions

If you have questions about the deletion process or need help with a request, contact us at:
yasartaymaz@windowslive.com
