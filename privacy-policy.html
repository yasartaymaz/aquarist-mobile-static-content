Privacy Policy

Aquarist - Aquarium Tracker & Log

Effective Date: March 27, 2026

Aquarist ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal aquarium tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Aquarist, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name used to create and manage your account.
  • Aquarium Data: Tank details, maintenance logs, water parameters, and other data you enter into the app.
  • Photos: Images you upload for your aquarium tanks. Photos are stored securely on our servers.
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers for push notifications, if you enable them.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features, including tank tracking, logging, and reminders.
  • To enable sharing features so you can share tank information with friends.
  • To send push notifications for maintenance reminders, if enabled.
  • To improve the app experience and fix issues.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud-based backend platform. We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level security policies, and secure authentication to protect your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your aquarium data is only shared with other users when you explicitly choose to use the app's sharing features.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and view all data associated with your account within the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Aquarist is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
