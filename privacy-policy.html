Privacy Policy

Aquarist - Aquarium Tracker & Log

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Aquarist ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal aquarium tracking application. It is developed and operated by Yaşar Taymaz, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the information described here. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, where it is kept, and what you can do about it.

What changed in version 2.0: the app no longer uses a third-party backend. Your data now lives on servers we run ourselves in Türkiye. Sign-in with a password has been removed and replaced with a one-time code sent to your email address.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, display name and language preference.
  • Aquarium data: tanks, maintenance logs, water parameters, reminders and anything else you type into the app.
  • Photos: images you upload for your tanks.
  • Push token: if you enable reminder notifications, a device-specific notification token.
  • Technical logs: our server records requests (including IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location or analytics about how you move through the app.

2. How You Sign In

There is no password. You enter your email address, we email you a one-time code, and you type that code in. You can also sign in with Apple or Google; in that case the provider confirms your identity to us and we receive your email address and a stable identifier, nothing else.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on our own servers, located in Türkiye, in a PostgreSQL database; photos are stored as files on the same servers. Connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the server is restricted to the developer.

Backups are taken daily and the most recent 14 backup sets are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.

4. Service Providers

We do not sell or trade your information, and we never share it for advertising. A small number of providers process data on our behalf so the app can work:

  • Resend — delivers the sign-in code emails. It receives your email address.
  • Expo push service, Apple (APNs) and Google (FCM) — deliver reminder notifications, if you enable them. They receive the notification token and the notification text.
  • Apple and Google — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Supabase — the backend used by version 1. Your data was migrated away from it and that copy is now read-only. It will be deleted once the migration is confirmed complete for all users.

5. Sharing a Tank

You can invite another person to a tank as a caretaker. They can see that tank and add logs and photos to it; they cannot see your other tanks or your account details. Reminders are private to each person. Removing a caretaker ends their access, while the logs and photos they added stay with the tank.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from Profile > Delete account. Deletion is reversible for 30 days: during that period the account is disabled and can be restored by signing in again. After 30 days the account, its tanks, logs and photos are permanently removed. Copies in backups disappear as those backups rotate out, within about two weeks.

7. Your Rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, and ask for it to be deleted. Most of this you can do inside the app; for anything else, write to the address below and we will answer.

8. Children's Privacy

Aquarist is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date; if a change is significant we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
